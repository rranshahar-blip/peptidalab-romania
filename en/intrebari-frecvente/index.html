What is an RUO product?

For research use only (RUO). Not for use in humans.

Can I purchase as an individual?

This decision has not yet been finalised. Eligibility will depend on each product’s classification and the applicable legal opinion.

Does the website provide medical advice?

No. The content does not replace a doctor, pharmacist, lawyer or compliance specialist.

Why are orders disabled?

Because legality, documentation, suppliers and the commercial process must be validated before money is accepted or products are shipped.

How do you verify purity?

We do not publish percentages until a competent laboratory has issued batch-linked results using an appropriate method.

Where can I find the COA?

On the product page, but only after validation. The staging version explicitly shows that it is unavailable.

Do you provide reconstitution instructions?

No. We do not publish dosing, injection, reconstitution or human-use instructions for RUO products.

How are products delivered?

The final policy will identify the carrier, timeframes and validated conditions. Nothing is currently being shipped.
