Scientific context

Why it is studied

DSIP is a nonapeptide historically described in sleep and neuroendocrine-rhythm research. Studies have explored its relationship with sleep architecture, stress responses and central signalling.

Experimental model

Investigated mechanism

There is no single accepted mechanism explaining all reported observations. Results vary with species, method and measured parameters.

Responsible interpretation

Evidence limitations

The evidence base is old and inconsistent, and human results are insufficient for therapeutic claims. The product is presented only as a research material.

This summary is informational and describes research context; it is not a medical claim or recommendation for use.
