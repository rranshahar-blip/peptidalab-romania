Scientific context

Why it is studied

PT-141, known as bremelanotide, is a melanocortin peptide investigated for activity at melanocortin receptors and central circuits associated with sexual behaviour.

Experimental model

Investigated mechanism

The principal model involves agonism at melanocortin receptors, including MC4R, and differs from the peripheral vascular mechanisms of other molecule classes.

Responsible interpretation

Evidence limitations

Bremelanotide exists as an active substance in an authorised medicine in certain jurisdictions. An RUO material is not equivalent to that product and is not intended for human use.

This summary is informational and describes research context; it is not a medical claim or recommendation for use.
