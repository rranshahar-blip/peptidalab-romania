Scientific context

Why it is studied

Somatropin is recombinant human growth hormone and is a protein, not a conventional RUO peptide. In research it is used to study the growth-hormone receptor and the GH–IGF-1 axis.

Experimental model

Investigated mechanism

Binding to the GH receptor activates cellular signalling pathways, including JAK2/STAT, with experimental effects that depend on tissue and model.

Responsible interpretation

Evidence limitations

Somatropin is a regulated medicinal active substance. Commercial availability remains blocked pending legal classification and verification of an authorised supply chain.

This summary is informational and describes research context; it is not a medical claim or recommendation for use.
